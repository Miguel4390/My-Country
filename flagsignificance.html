What Is The Meaning Behind The Flag?

The Colors and Emblem:

The Mexican flag has 3 colors, and while they may seem random or hold no purpose it is actually quite the oppsite.
The three colors, Green, White, and Red, infact hold a more meainful truth. The color Green is meant to stand for Hope, while
White is for unity and Red for the blood of all the fallen heros. The Emblem of a eagle eaing a snake, in the middle of the flag,
dates back to an old aztec legend about finding the right place to build their home.
