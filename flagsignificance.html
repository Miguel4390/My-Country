What Is The Meaning Behind The Flag?

The Colors and Emblem:

The Mexican flag has 3 colors, and while they may seem random or hold no purpose, it is very much oppsite.
The three colors, Green, White, and Red, infact hold a more meainful truth. The color Green is meant to stand for Hope, while
White is for unity and Red for the blood of all the fallen heros. The Emblem of a eagle eaing a snake, in the middle of the flag,
dates back to an old aztec legend about finding the right place to build their home. These hidden meanings in the flag are what
make it unique, and they dont just stop in with the colors and the eagle ans snake.

The Cactus

The cactus, respresents the rocky island and harsh landscape the Aztecs first had to face when building their home. It also
serves as a symbol, which highlights all of the dangerous and difficult trial the Aztecs had to overcome.

The Background

Besides the cactus there are other meainful symbols, such as the Oak and laurel branches which are tied under the cactus. The Oak
branch is meant to stand for strength, while the Laurel Branches are suppose to be seen as a symbol of victory and honor for all
of the National Heros.
